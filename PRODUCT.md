# Product truth — Alessandro Arenas

## Purpose

A personal portfolio and contact site for Alessandro Arenas, an independent digital solutions specialist based in La Serena, Chile. The site explains his services and invites prospective clients to describe a business need and continue the conversation through WhatsApp.

## Audience and context

Owners of Chilean small and medium businesses, especially technical services, construction, workshops, and trades. The site should use direct Spanish, explain services without unnecessary jargon, and make scope and next steps understandable.

## Services presented

- Professional websites and landing pages.
- Software adapted to a business operation, including customer, quotation, and work order processes.
- Process automation connecting existing forms, email, spreadsheets, and systems.
- AI-assisted tasks such as classifying inquiries, reading documents, and supervised assistants, when appropriate.

## Evidence on the site

- Ventora is a separate product developed by Alessandro, focused on commercial quotations for glass, aluminum, and PVC businesses. Its site is `https://ventorap.cl`.
- ICT Zella is presented as a digital trading journal. Its site is `https://martin-trader-ict-zella.vercel.app/`.
- No customer testimonials, quantified outcomes, or product screenshots have been supplied as verified evidence for this redesign.

## Contact and privacy

The contact form prepares a message in the visitor's browser and hands it to WhatsApp. The commercial WhatsApp number is owner-configured in the public `sitio/config.js`; it must not be invented or represented as configured if it is empty. Preserve existing validation, consent, and privacy policy behavior.

## Brand commitments for this redesign

- The approved visual direction is “Atelier Nocturno: tecnología editorial de autor.”
- Use charcoal `#101311`, ivory `#F3F0E9`, sage `#92A796`, deep green `#4A5B51`, and warm gray `#A6A9A3`.
- Use Instrument Serif for editorial titles and italic emphasis, with Manrope for navigation, body copy, controls, and forms.
- Present Ventora as the primary portfolio case and ICT Zella as a secondary case.
- Keep the experience personal and professional rather than implying a large agency.
- Keep the site static in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Do not alter Ventora or ICT Zella, add paid services, invent claims, publish, or merge to `main`.
